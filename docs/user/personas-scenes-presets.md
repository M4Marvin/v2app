# Personas, scenes, and presets

A persona is who you are in a conversation: a name, a short description, and an optional icon. A scene is a picture shown behind a chat for atmosphere. A preset is a saved bundle of generation settings you can reuse. This page covers all three, plus the display options you can turn on per chat or for the whole app. Everything here lives in the settings, and nothing needs a technical skill.

## Personas

Open any chat, click the gear icon in the chat header, and choose the **Persona** tab. That's where personas live.

To create one, give it a **Name** (required) and an optional **Description**. You can upload an **Icon**, a PNG, but only when editing the persona later. Charon comes with a **Default** persona from day one, and you can edit or delete that one too.

The **Active persona** is account-wide. It applies to all your chats, not just the one you're in. Your active persona shows as your avatar in chats, and it's the voice used when you impersonate. A persona does not change the AI. It changes how the AI sees you, and how you appear in the story.

## Scenes

A scene is a background image shown behind a chat for atmosphere. In chat Settings, choose the **Scene** tab to see the library. Each chat keeps its own choice.

Pick a background and it applies to that chat only. Click the same one again to deselect it. **Clear scene** removes it entirely. As the admin, you can upload your own background images and delete ones you've added. Charon ships with no default backgrounds, so the library starts empty.

## Presets

Presets are bundles of generation settings. Go to **Settings**, then open **Presets**, to manage them.

Create a preset with a **Name**. You can bind it to a **Provider** and a **Model**, then set these values:

- **Temperature**: higher means more creative, lower means more focused.
- **Top P**: nucleus sampling, which narrows the AI's word choices.
- **Max tokens**: how long a reply can be.
- **Context size**: how much conversation the AI can see.
- **Frequency penalty**: discourages repeating the same words.
- **Presence penalty**: encourages talking about new topics.

Presets apply to new chats, and you can switch presets per chat. You can duplicate, edit, or delete any preset, and set one as your default. If you keep more than one provider, binding a preset to a specific one keeps those settings from leaking into the wrong model.

## Display options

**Settings** has a **Preferences** page with three toggles that change how messages look:

- **Highlight dialogue**: colors quoted dialogue.
- **Auto-fix Markdown**: repairs common formatting slips.
- **Block external media**: stops messages from loading outside images or videos.

These affect how messages appear while you read, not how the AI writes. The same three toggles exist per chat under the **Display** tab in chat Settings, and you can change them at any time.

## Profile

The **Profile** page under **Settings** is where you change your password. Changing it signs out your other sessions. Your username stays the same.

## Command menu

Press **Ctrl+K**, or click the **Search** button in the header, to jump straight to Characters, Chats, Lorebooks, or Settings. You can also create things from there, like **Import character** or **New lorebook**. It's the fastest way to move around, and it works from anywhere in the app.
